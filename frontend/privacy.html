Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Draft pending review by qualified legal counsel. This page describes what the service actually does today; it is not yet legal advice or a final policy.

Who we are, and who this service is for

KathaSajha creates illustrated children's stories from ideas that parents and guardians type in. Accounts are for adults. Children do not create accounts, do not log in, and cannot submit information to us directly. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to use KathaSajha with you.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, a password (stored only as a bcrypt hash, never as text), and an optional display name.
  • Stories: the ideas you type, the stories and illustrations generated from them, and — optionally — a child's first name if you choose to make them the hero. We ask you not to include surnames, birthdays, addresses, or any other identifying details about a child in prompts or names.
  • Usage and technical data: counts of stories created (used to enforce plan limits), timestamps, and request logs that include IP addresses, kept for security and abuse prevention.
  • Payment: if you subscribe, payment is processed by Stripe. We never see or store card numbers — only Stripe's identifiers for your customer record and subscription status.

Children's information

The only child-related information we handle is what a parent chooses to type: a first name and a story idea. It is provided by the parent, used solely to personalise that family's stories, never used for advertising, and deleted with the account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children themselves. If you believe a child has submitted information to us directly, contact us and we will delete it.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • Google (Gemini API): your story idea and the child's first name, if provided, are sent to Google's generative AI service to write and illustrate the story.
  • Stripe: subscription billing.
  • Our hosting provider: runs the servers and database.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

A story is private to your account until you create a share link. Anyone with the link can read that story and download its book, without an account. You can revoke a link at any time from the story page: the shared page and book download stop working immediately. Illustration files already loaded may remain in browser or network caches, and anyone who saved an image's direct address could still reach that image until the story itself is deleted.

How long we keep things

Account data and stories are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Generation records — a timestamp, which AI provider was used, and how much it cost — are retained as financial records; when you delete your account these are detached from you, keeping no reference to your account or your stories. Security logs containing IP addresses are retained for up to 30 days.

Deleting your account

Use Delete my account in the site footer while logged in. It removes your account, every story and illustration, and any child names from the live service immediately, and asks our payment processor to cancel any active subscription. Copies may persist in encrypted database backups until those backups rotate out (currently within 30 days). Anonymous generation records — counts and timestamps with no link to you — are kept as financial records. You can also request deletion by email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or erase your personal data, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise any of them.
