The service

KathaSajha generates illustrated children's stories from ideas you provide. You must be an adult to create an account. You are responsible for what you type into prompts and for reviewing stories before reading them to a child — generative AI can make mistakes.

Acceptable use

Don't use KathaSajha to attempt to generate content that is unsafe for children, to include personal details of any child beyond a first name, to infringe others' rights (including requesting characters you don't own), or to probe, overload, or abuse the service. We may suspend accounts that do.

Your stories

You own the stories created from your ideas, to the extent the law allows ownership of AI-generated content, and you can download and share them freely. We store them only to show them to you and the people you share them with.

Plans and billing

The free plan includes a monthly story allowance. Paid plans are billed monthly through Stripe and can be cancelled any time from billing settings, taking effect at the end of the paid period. Allowances that protect us from abuse are described on the pricing page and may evolve; paid entitlements will not shrink mid-period.

Availability and warranty

The service is provided "as is", without warranty. We work to keep it available and to fail softly — a failed generation is not charged against your allowance — but we cannot promise uninterrupted service.

Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the amount you paid us in the twelve months before the claim.

Ending the agreement

You can delete your account at any time from the site footer, which removes your data as described in the Privacy Policy.
